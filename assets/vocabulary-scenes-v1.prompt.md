# Vocabulary scene atlas

Built-in image generation. Garden hero used as style reference only. Panels are ordered decide, borrow, advice, avoid, explain, enough in three columns by two rows. UI crops by CSS, without modifying the source image or course assets.

## Prompt

Create one illustrated sprite atlas for an English vocabulary lesson, landscape 3:2 aspect ratio, exactly 3 equal square columns by 2 equal square rows, edge-to-edge panels with no gutters, borders or captions. Reference is STYLE ONLY: polished sunny hand-painted cartoon, warm ink contours, rich but calm colors, soft volumetric light, matching this learning garden. Six everyday scenes, same friendly young adult female protagonist wearing a teal shirt, no words anywhere. TOP LEFT: decide, protagonist choosing between a hiking backpack and a picnic basket, pointing decisively to picnic basket. TOP MIDDLE: borrow, seated protagonist holding a phone with empty battery pictogram, friend handing her a charging cable to borrow, clear handoff toward protagonist. TOP RIGHT: advice, friend giving thoughtful practical guidance to protagonist preparing a job interview, resume paper with abstract lines only, supportive conversation gesture. BOTTOM LEFT: avoid, protagonist choosing a clear quiet walking route away from an obvious traffic jam, visible busy cars on the other path. BOTTOM MIDDLE: explain, friend explaining a simple board game's rules to protagonist, pointing at board pieces. BOTTOM RIGHT: enough, protagonist checking a full water jug and six filled glasses for a six-person picnic, visibly sufficient water. All figures and meaningful objects contained in their own square panel with breathing room around edges. No words, letters, labels, numbers, speech-bubble text, UI, watermark. Each scene must convey the action naturally; do not add decorative flowers that obscure the teaching action.
