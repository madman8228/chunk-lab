在家中 · 休息日～理财

本课程收录 89 句日常英语。每句都按意群切分，配中文释义、逐段 AI 详解与词卡，点开任意一句即可看到完整拆解。

本课程全部句子

  • I want to take a nap. 我真想睡个午觉。
  • I'm going to lie down. 我去躺一会儿。
  • You're pretending to be asleep. 你在装睡啊!
  • Were you sleeping? 你睡着了吗?
  • No, I was awake. 没有，还没睡呢。
  • Will you change the baby's diaper? 你能给孩子换块尿布吗?
  • Do you need to pee? 想尿尿吗?
  • It's time to go wee-wee. 该尿尿了。
  • Peekaboo! 孩子们在玩藏猫游戏时常用。大人有时也半带玩乐地使用。
  • Kuchi-kuchi-koo. 胳肢胳肢
  • Let's play catch. 我们来投球吧。
  • The water is leaking. 漏水了。
  • It's so dusty. 全是灰呀。
  • It's stuffy in this room. 这间屋子通风真差。
  • It's drafty in this room. 这间房漏风。
  • Will you feed the dog? 你能去喂喂狗吗?
  • Will you take the dog for a walk? 你去遛遛狗吧?
  • Take care of my brother and sister. 帮我照看一下弟弟和妹妹。
  • Please water the plants. 请(给植物)浇点水。
  • What a mess! 啊!怎么这么乱呀!
  • Help me. 帮帮我吧。
  • Clean up your room. 把你的屋子收拾收拾。
  • Help me clean up the house. 帮我打扫打扫卫生。
  • We're out of dish detergent. 洗涤灵用完啦。
  • Would you put up the clothes to dry? 你能把衣服晾上吗?
  • Will you help me fold up the clothes? 你能帮我把衣服叠起来吗?
  • Please sweep the floor. 把地扫扫。
  • Please scrub the sink. 把厨房的池子洗干净。
  • I have to vacuum my room. 我得用吸尘器吸吸我房间了。
  • Please dust the shelves. 请把架子上的灰掸掉。
  • Please mop the floor. 请拖拖地。
  • Will you iron the shirt? 你能把那件衬衫熨熨吗?
  • I have to iron my skirt. 我的裙子得熨了。
  • Let's go grocery shopping. 我们去超市买东西吧。
  • The park was crowded. 公园里人挤人。
  • Can you baby-sit tonight? 今晚能帮我照看一下孩子吗?
  • This is for you. 这是送给你的。
  • This is your share. 这是你的那份。
  • What do you want for your birthday? 你生日想要什么礼物？
  • Ta-dah! 噢，来喽!
  • I usually work out after work. 我通常下班后运动。
  • I've started jogging. 我开始慢跑锻炼。
  • Do you dream often? 你常做梦吗?
  • I've been forgetful lately. 最近我总是丢三落四的。
  • When is this due? 什么时候到期?
  • It's due on the thirtieth. 交费日期截止到30号。
  • Could you give me change? 能帮我换一下零钱吗?
  • Do you have change for one hundred yen? 能帮我换开100日元吗?
  • I need to deposit five thousand yen in my savings account. 我要存5000日元(在我的账户上。)
  • I need to withdraw ¥5,000 from my savings account. 我要取5000日元(从我的账户上)。
  • I paid out of my own pocket. 我是自己掏的腰包。
  • I'm out of cash. 我没带现金。
  • I don't have much money on me now. 我现在没有多少现金。
  • I'm broke. 身无分文。
  • I have a lot of money on me now. 现在我有很多现金。
  • I can't afford to be lazy. 我可没有时间闲呆着。
  • What a waste! 多浪费呀!
  • He didn't pay the debt and disappeared. 他因为还不上债而躲了起来。
  • I'd rather stay in than go out tonight. 我今晚宁愿待在家里也不愿出去。
  • I'm looking for an apartment near the university. 我在找大学附近的公寓。
  • How much is the rent for this apartment? 这套公寓的租金是多少？
  • Does the rent include the utilities? 房租包含水电燃气这些费用吗？
  • I need to sign a one-year lease. 我需要签一份一年期的租约。
  • I can't wait to move into my new place. 我迫不及待要搬进新家了。
  • Do you still have the apartment for rent? 这套公寓还在出租吗？
  • I'd like to open a savings account. 我想开一个储蓄账户。
  • Could you tell me today's exchange rate? 你能告诉我今天的汇率吗？
  • How much would it cost to send this to China? 把这个寄到中国要多少钱？
  • I'd like to get a haircut, please. 我想理个发，麻烦你了。
  • Could you trim my beard a little? 你能帮我把胡子修短一点吗？
  • Could you take a little off the top? 能把头顶稍微剪短点吗？
  • I go to the gym twice a week. 我每周去两次健身房。
  • I'm really out of shape these days. 我最近身体状况真的很差。
  • The Wi-Fi keeps dropping in my room. 我房间的无线网络老是断。
  • Could I borrow your charger for a sec? 我能借用一下你的充电器吗？
  • My phone is running out of battery. 我的手机快没电了。
  • I've got dandruff. 我有头皮屑。
  • Try some shampoo. 试试洗发水。
  • What's the rent? 房租多少？
  • 300 a week. 一周三百。
  • Get some fresh air. 去呼吸点新鲜空气。
  • Get some sun. 去晒晒太阳。
  • Get some groceries. 买点菜。
  • Take a walk. 去走走。
  • Read a book. 读读书。
  • Put on some music. 放点音乐。
  • Smell some flowers. 闻闻花香。
  • Have a glass of wine. 喝一杯酒。
  • Have some nice food. 吃点好的。
