见面、分手时 · 有关学校～有关年龄、身高和体重

本课程收录 74 句日常英语。每句都按意群切分，配中文释义、逐段 AI 详解与词卡，点开任意一句即可看到完整拆解。

本课程全部句子

  • Are you a student? 你是学生吗?
  • I'm a college student. 我是个大学生。
  • I'm studying English. 我正在学英语。
  • I went to Meiji University. 我上的是明治大学。
  • Where did you go to college? 你在哪儿上的大学？
  • What school do you go to? 你上什么学校?
  • I go to...university. 我上……大学。
  • What year are you in? 你上几年级了?
  • I'm a freshman. 我上一年级。
  • I'll graduate next year. 我明年毕业。
  • What's your major? 你的专业是什么?
  • I'm an English major. 我是英语专业的。
  • What club are you in? 你参加哪个俱乐部?
  • I'm in the ski club. 我在滑雪俱乐部。
  • Do you have a part-time job? 你有兼职工作吗?
  • I work at a bookstore as a cashier once a week. 我一个星期去一次书店做收银员。
  • What are your plans after graduation? 毕业以后你打算做什么?
  • How many people are in your family? 你家有几口人?
  • Four people. 四口人。
  • My parents and my younger sister. 父母和我的妹妹。
  • Any brothers or sisters? 你有兄弟姐妹吗?
  • Just one brother. 只有一个哥哥(弟弟)。
  • Do you live with your parents? 你和父母住在一起吗?
  • I live alone. 我一个人住。
  • I live in an apartment. 我住在公寓里。
  • Are you married? 你结婚了吗?
  • No, not yet. 不，还没有。
  • I have a fiancé. 我有未婚夫/未婚妻了。
  • I'm going to get married next year. 我打算明年结婚。
  • I'm married. 我结婚了。
  • I'm engaged. 我订婚了。
  • Any children? 有孩子吗?
  • I have two boys. 我有两个男孩。
  • I have one daughter in elementary school. 我有一个女儿，在上小学。
  • I don't have any children. 我没有孩子。
  • What are your hobbies? 你有什么爱好?
  • Do you have any hobbies? 你有什么爱好吗?
  • What do you do when you have free time? 业余时间你都干什么?
  • I like to watch movies. 我喜欢看电影。
  • What kind of movies do you like? 你喜欢看什么样的电影?
  • You're a good pianist. 你的钢琴弹得真好。
  • What kind of sports do you like? 你喜欢哪种运动?
  • How long have you been skiing? 你滑了多长时间雪了?
  • I just like to watch. 我只是喜欢看。
  • I'm a baseball fan. 我是棒球迷。
  • I like to play golf. 我喜欢打高尔夫球。
  • Have you ever done aerobics? 你练过健美操吗?
  • What are your interests? 你对什么感兴趣?
  • I've never done that. 我从没做过。
  • Have you ever traveled abroad? 你去国外旅行过吗?
  • I've been to the U.S. and Germany. 我去美国和德国了。
  • Where do you want to go next? 下次你想去哪儿?
  • How old are you? 你多大了?
  • I'm forty-eight. 我48岁。
  • How much do you weigh? 你的体重是多少?
  • About seventy-five kilograms. 75公斤左右。
  • I weigh a-hundred-sixty-four pounds. 我重164磅。
  • How tall are you? 你有多高?
  • About one-hundred-eighty centimeters. 1米80左右。
  • I'm five feet three inches tall. 我身高5英尺3寸。
  • Do you enjoy watching movies? 你喜欢看电影吗？
  • I'm pretty good at cooking. 我挺擅长做饭的。
  • I usually go for a run in the morning. 我通常早上跑步。
  • I'm into cooking these days. 我最近迷上做饭了。
  • May I hand in my homework tomorrow? 我明天交作业可以吗？
  • I'm having trouble understanding this part. 我理解这部分有困难。
  • Could you explain it in a simpler way? 你能用更简单的方式解释吗？
  • I'll try my best to catch up with the class. 我会尽最大努力赶上班级进度。
  • I used to play the piano when I was little. 我小时候常常弹钢琴。
  • Did you get taller? 你长高了吗？
  • You are growing fast. 你长得真快。
  • Twenty something. 二十多岁吧。
  • I'm trying to study. 我在学习。
  • My test is tomorrow. 我明天有考试。
