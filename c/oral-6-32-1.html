在外用餐时 · 在快餐厅里～点菜

本课程收录 63 句日常英语。每句都按意群切分，配中文释义、逐段 AI 详解与词卡，点开任意一句即可看到完整拆解。

本课程全部句子

  • I'd like a hamburger and an iced tea. 要一个汉堡和一杯冰茶。
  • May I have two hot dogs, please? 我要两个热狗。
  • With ketchup and mustard, please. 请加蕃茄酱和芥末。
  • Will that be for here or to go? 您是在这儿用餐还是打包带走？
  • Will that be to go? 您带走吗?
  • Will you be eating here? 您是在这儿吃吗?
  • To go, please. 带走。
  • For here, please. 在这儿吃。
  • Could you recommend a good restaurant near here? 这附近您能推荐家好吃的饭馆吗?
  • I'd like some Italian food. 我想吃意大利菜。
  • Which restaurant do you recommend? 您推荐哪家饭馆?
  • Is there a Mexican restaurant around here? 这附近有墨西哥餐馆吗?
  • Where is the closest Mexican restaurant? 最近的墨西哥餐馆在哪儿?
  • Are there any restaurants still open near here? 这附近有没有还在营业的饭馆?
  • Do I need a reservation? 我需要预订吗?
  • Hello, this is the Park Restaurant. May I help you? 您好，这是公园餐厅。我可以为您服务吗?
  • I'd like to make a reservation for eight people at six o'clock tonight. 我想订个座位，今晚六点、八位。
  • We are a group of eight. 我们一共8个人。
  • I'd like to reserve a table for eight. 我想预订一张八人桌。
  • Could we have a table close to the band? 我们能不能要张离乐队近一点儿的桌子?
  • I'm sorry. We're all booked up tonight. 抱歉，今晚已经订满了。
  • How long is the wait? 要等多长时间?
  • We can wait. 我们可以等。
  • What time can we make a reservation? 可以预订几点的?
  • How late are you open? 一直营业到几点?
  • How do I get there? 我怎么才能到那儿?
  • Do you accept credit cards? 可以用信用卡吗?
  • What is the price of a dinner course? 一份晚餐套餐多少钱？
  • What is your dress code? 衣着上有什么规定?
  • Should I wear a jacket and tie? 我必须穿夹克、打领带吗？
  • Should the ladies wear dresses? 女士们需要穿连衣裙吗？
  • How many in your group? 请问您几位?
  • Two. 2个人。
  • I'm sorry, but I have to cancel my reservation. 抱歉，我得取消预订。
  • Could I have a menu, please? 请给我菜单。
  • Do you have a menu in Japanese? 有日语的菜单吗?
  • May I see the wine list, please? 请给我看一下酒单。
  • What kind of wine do you have? 你们都有些什么葡萄酒?
  • May I take your order? 我可以为您点单了吗？
  • Can I have the same as that? 我想要和那个一样的。
  • I'll take this and this. 我要这个和这个。
  • Do you have any local specialties? 您这儿有什么地方风味吗?
  • Could we have separate checks? 我们可以分别付款吗?
  • I'd like to have dinner for sixty dollars including drinks. 我要一个60美元的晚餐包括酒水。
  • I'd like a steak. 我要一份牛排。
  • I'd like some red wine, please. 我要红葡萄酒。
  • How would you like it? 要几成熟的?
  • Well-done, please. 烤熟点。
  • Can you make it mild? 请做得清淡些。
  • Are you having breakfast? 您用早餐吗?
  • Is coffee included with this meal? 套餐里包括咖啡吗?
  • I'd like a cup of coffee, please. 请给我来一杯咖啡。
  • Anything else? 还要别的吗?
  • That's all for me. 够了/就要这些。
  • I didn't order this. 我没有点这个菜。
  • My order hasn't come yet. 我点的菜还没上。
  • Could we get the menu, please? 请给我们菜单好吗？
  • I'd like a medium latte, please. 我要一杯中杯拿铁，谢谢。
  • You should definitely try this place. 你一定要试试这家店。
  • I'd like the steak, medium rare, please. 我要牛排，五分熟，谢谢。
  • Do you have any tables available for two? 现在有两人位吗？
  • I'll have a burger. 我要一个汉堡。
  • That's everything. 就这些。
