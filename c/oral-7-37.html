结束部分

本课程收录 22 句日常英语。每句都按意群切分，配中文释义、逐段 AI 详解与词卡，点开任意一句即可看到完整拆解。

本课程全部句子

  • We would appreciate an early reply. 如能尽早回复，我们将不胜感激。
  • We look forward to your favorable reply. 我们期待着您满意的回答。
  • We look forward to hearing from you soon. 我们盼望着不久能听到您的回音。
  • Your prompt attention to this matter will be appreciated. 如您能尽快处理此事，我们将不胜感激。
  • Please write us at the above address. 请按上述地址给我们写信。
  • Your kind cooperation in this respect is greatly appreciated. 非常感谢您在这方面的配合。
  • We hope we can count on your continued cooperation and support. 我们希望能够继续得到你们的合作和支持。
  • We look forward to your... 我们期待着您的……
  • We trust our request will... 我们确信我们的请求将……
  • We are looking forward to the time when... 我们期待着当……的那一刻。
  • Please do not hesitate to contact us. 请您不必客气，尽管与我们联系。
  • We are always anxious to assist you in... 我们随时乐意在……方面为您提供帮助。
  • Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. 如果您有什么问题的话，请不必客气，尽管与我们联系。
  • Thank you for calling this matter to our attention. 谢谢您提醒我们注意此事。
  • Thank you very much for your cooperation. 非常感谢您给予我们的合作。
  • Thank you for the opportunity to be of service. 感谢您给我们这个为您服务的机会。
  • We appreciate this opportunity to... 我们感谢能有这样的机会去……
  • It is always a pleasure doing business with you. 与您合作一直很愉快。
  • Please accept our sincere apologies. 请接受我们诚挚的歉意。
  • I want to extend my personal apologies for... 我以我个人的身份为……向您赔礼道歉。
  • We wish you a bright and successful New Year. 我们预祝您在新的一年里将更加辉煌和成功。
  • We trust that an increase in ... will continue in the future. 我们相信……会继续增长。
